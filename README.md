# AI-Powered Construction Daily Report Automation

A workflow automation built by **HerCode LLC** to reduce the manual work involved in construction daily reporting and turn field updates into structured, actionable information.

## The Problem

Construction teams collect important information from the field every day: work completed, labor activity, delays, blockers, weather conditions, materials, and other project updates.

When that information is manually organized, reviewed, logged, and emailed, the process can become repetitive and time consuming while making it harder for management to quickly see what is happening across a project.

## The Solution

HerCode LLC designed an end-to-end workflow that takes a field submission and automatically moves the information through the reporting process.

### Workflow

**Field Submission → Data Capture → AI Analysis → Management Log → Email Report**

1. A team member submits the daily field report through an online form.
2. Submission data is captured in a centralized spreadsheet.
3. The automation detects the new submission and sends the field data for AI-powered analysis.
4. The analysis organizes the information into a structured daily report.
5. Key information is recorded in a management log.
6. The completed report is automatically delivered by email.

## Report Analysis

The workflow can organize submitted field information into sections such as:

- Daily report summary
- Work completed
- Labor activity
- Delays and blockers
- Weather impact
- Material or resource concerns
- Management visibility and follow-up items

## Business Value

This workflow is designed to:

- Reduce repetitive administrative work
- Standardize daily reporting
- Improve visibility into field activity
- Surface delays and blockers faster
- Create organized management records
- Reduce the time between field reporting and management updates

## Technology & Integration

The solution combines:

`Workflow Automation` `AI` `Google Forms` `Google Sheets` `OpenAI` `Gmail`

The workflow was designed and tested as a working end-to-end automation, from the initial field submission through the final management email.
